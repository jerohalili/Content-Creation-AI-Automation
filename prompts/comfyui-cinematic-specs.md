# ComfyUI Image Engineering Specs
**Target Model:** Z Image Turbo (Optimized for photorealism).

## The 4-Layer Prompt Structure
1. **SUBJECT:** Filipino professionals only. Focus on natural skin tones and authentic features.
2. **ENVIRONMENT:** Specific PH locations (BGC high-rise, Makati co-working, BPO floors).
3. **LIGHTING:** Specific quality (soft window light, warm golden hour, or monitor glow).
4. **LENS/COMPOSITION:** Cinematic feels (85mm shallow DOF, 24mm wide, or macro keyboard shots).

## Hard Constraints
- **Zero Text:** No logos, UI labels, or words allowed in the generated image.
- **No Stock Aesthetic:** Avoid generic, overly-sanitized "corporate" looks.
